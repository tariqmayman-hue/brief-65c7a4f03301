Key note
Musalem's 19:40 speech is a HARD event (a policy Fed speech), so don't enter right before it. The 30Y auction at 19:00 can swing yields either way.
What's driving it
  • Oil shock: Brent is around $104.6 (+4.4%) and WTI around $92.2. Reports say Trump is weighing a strike on Iran before the midterms, and there have been new tanker attacks. Hurricane Isaias has shut about a quarter of US Gulf oil output. Higher oil means hotter inflation, which keeps Fed hikes on the table and hurts stocks.
  • Yields: The 10Y is about 5.32% and the 30Y about 5.71%, both up around 4bp. Waller (a Fed voter) said this morning that more hikes are needed. High long yields hit the Dow, small caps and rate-sensitive sectors hardest.
  • Tech: Nasdaq futures are soft on worries about how AI spending is being financed, such as Broadcom reportedly looking for $50B+ in funding. Asia closed lower, with the Nikkei -1.4% and Hang Seng -2.1%.

Invalidation — what flips the call

The bearish call is wrong if the Iran-strike talk cools, oil fades, and a strong 30Y auction (like yesterday's 10Y) pulls yields lower. If that happens, watch for indices to reclaim PDC (US30 51,106, NAS100 31,190, SPX 7,801), which would move the bias to Neutral.

Bottom line

Oil and yields set the tone today, so lean bearish. Thursday is a focus day: wait for a 1H swing-failure setup at a level, stay out right before Musalem, and be flat by session end.
