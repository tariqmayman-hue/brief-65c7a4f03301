Cross-asset coherence: do equities, rates, oil, credit and crypto tell the same story?
Fri 9 Oct 2026NY Pre-Market Brief · 14:25 SAST
Neutral
Desk view

The cross-asset picture is a relief bounce: oil −1.1%, yields flat near highs, dollar softer, gold +1.35%, Nasdaq futures +0.81%, BTC +1.1%. It's consistent but not a new trend: Fed hikes are still priced and Iran risk is only paused until 3 Nov.

Analysis
Transmission chain
  • Trump's no-strike pledge → oil lower → less inflation pressure → yields flat → tech bounce.
  • Lower yields and a softer dollar → gold and silver higher.
  • Hot inflation or a hawkish Fed → yields up → Dow and rate-sensitive sectors lose first.
