Key note
HARD event today: Collins (policy Fed speech) at 22:00 SAST, so don't enter right before it. UMich at 16:00 is WATCH only: its inflation expectations feed Fed hike odds.
What's driving it
  • Oil eases: Brent is about $103.14 (−1.1%) and WTI about $90.49 (−1.1%) after Trump said the US won't attack Iran before the 3 Nov midterms, citing productive talks. The US blockade stays, Iran is still reviewing the Hormuz plan, and Hurricane Isaias has shut in about 63% of US Gulf oil output, so the supply risk is not gone.
  • AI rebound: Nasdaq futures are +0.81% after Thursday's AI selloff, which followed an FT report that OpenAI's annualised revenue is about $20B below what was signalled. Oracle and Broadcom are up about 1.5% and CoreWeave about 2.5% premarket. Earnings season starts next week with the banks.
  • Fed hikes and yields: The Fed raised rates to 3.75-4.00% on 16 Sept. Waller and Musalem both said on Thursday that more hikes are likely. The 10Y yield is about 5.24%, near the 24-year high of 5.364% from Wednesday, and markets price about an 83% chance of a December hike.
  • Asia: Hang Seng +1.8% (tech index +3%), ASX 200 +0.6%, Nikkei flat (−0.02%). Seoul and Taipei were closed for holidays.
